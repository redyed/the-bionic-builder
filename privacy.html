1. Who We Are

Sovereign Architect LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Bionic Builder website and related services. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you join our waitlist, request resources, or interact with our site.

When you join the waitlist or request The Ownership Ladder, we collect:

  • Email address

We also collect standard technical data via analytics (pages viewed, referrer, device type) through Vercel Analytics and, when active, Meta Pixel. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally by name.

3. How We Use Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Email Communications

By joining the waitlist you consent to receive emails from Sovereign Architect LLC. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You may also reply directly — we read replies.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies (Vercel Analytics). When Meta Pixel is installed it will set additional cookies for advertising measurement and Lead event tracking. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Storage & Security

Email addresses are stored with our email service provider under industry-standard security practices. We retain waitlist data until you unsubscribe or request deletion, or until the program concludes and data is no longer needed.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
